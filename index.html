Doy Din Dang

The pottery
that started it all

Nestled in the hills of Northern Thailand, Doy Din Dang meaning red clay hill is a forest studio founded by artist Somluk Pantiboon, who trained under master potters in Japan before returning to Chiang Rai to build something truly his own. Every piece here is handmade using local raw materials, blending daily-life functionality with contemporary artistry. Located about 12km north of Chiang Rai, the studio features a large showroom and a hidden coffee shop tucked behind it.

Friendcation - Specialty Coffee and Eatery

Where we
called it home

Friendcation Specialty Coffee and Eatery is more than just a cafe. Tucked away in Chiang Rai with sweeping mountain views, beautifully landscaped gardens, and a stunning white arched building, Friendcation welcomes you into a world of warmth and good vibes. Inside, the signature mint green interior, floating flowers, La Marzocco espresso machine, and the glowing neon reminder that life is beautiful and so are you set the tone perfectly.

The Interview

Meeting the
founder

As part of our agile marketing project, we had the opportunity to sit down with the founder of Friendcation Cafe for an in-depth interview. The conversation gave us invaluable insights into the vision behind the cafe, the story of how it came to be, and what makes Friendcation truly special in Chiang Rai.

The founders passion and openness made our research come alive. We are grateful for their time and trust in our team.
